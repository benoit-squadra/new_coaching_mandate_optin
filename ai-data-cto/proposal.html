About your AI and data startup

An AI and data startup, now in hypergrowth

The tech team went from 5 people in September to 15 in November, and should double again within 3 to 6 months. It works across two sites, with part of the team in France and part in San Francisco. The co-founder and CTO is very smart: the gap is not technical.

The challenge

Structure and run a team growing this fast

The CTO needs an experienced sparring partner who has already been through hypergrowth or led a large team, with strong management skills and a real grasp of AI.

  • → Back the CTO, not replace them: work alongside them on how the team is organised and led
  • → Design the org for 30 engineers: team leads, hiring rhythm, onboarding and rituals
  • → Run one team across France and San Francisco, with one culture and one way of working
  • → Bring hands-on AI depth that earns the CTO's trust quickly
Timeline

Three months to set the structure, then rolling monthly.

Tentative start in January 2027, 2 to 3 days per week. The sequencing below is indicative: we firm it up with the CTO and the selected member during the discovery call.

MONTH 1

Diagnose

Time with the CTO, the leads and both sites: how decisions are made, where the team slows down, what breaks at 30.

MONTH 2

Design

Agree the target org with the CTO: team topology, leadership roles, hiring plan and the rituals that link France and SF.

MONTH 3

Embed

Put the new structure to work, coach the CTO and new leads through it, and agree what support looks like from there.
