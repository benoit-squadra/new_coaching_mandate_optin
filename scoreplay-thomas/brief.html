01 · Client context

Who Thomas is and what he's stepping into.

About ScorePlay. ScorePlay sits at the intersection of technology, media and sports. Its AI platform automatically tags and organises sports content, so clubs and leagues can publish highlights and stories in minutes rather than hours. It works with more than 200 sports organisations worldwide, including NBA and NHL franchises, Major League Soccer and the NWSL. The company was founded in 2021 and raised a $13M Series A backed by Seven Seven Six, Giannis Antetokounmpo and others. It is profitable and growing fast, and now building its US presence.

About Thomas. Thomas Plessis is Head of Sales EMEA, based in Biarritz. He leads a team of eleven, expected to reach thirteen or fifteen next year as the US market comes in. The next step is to become a manager of managers, which means moving from strong individual contributor to genuine leader of leaders. ScorePlay's HR Business Partner, Tamara Tomanic, is backing the process and wants a coach focused on the personal and leadership side, not a sales mentor.

How the call went. Thomas and Benoit spoke on 2 November. Thomas sees a coach as a GPS, a way to step back and structure how he manages, without looking for ready-made business solutions. He names team cohesion, a warm atmosphere and eloquence as his strengths, and organisation, planning and rigour as the areas he keeps working on. He agreed to go ahead and will choose two coaches for a discovery call. Chemistry will decide.

02 · The coaching need

Five things that make this mandate specific.

From team lead to manager of managers

With a team set to grow from eleven to thirteen or fifteen, Thomas has to move from contributor to true leader. The coach helps him change posture, not just skills.

Confidence in a bigger role

He wants to feel fully legitimate in the role and make sound decisions under pressure, with a leadership posture he trusts.

Presenting to an audience with ease

Thomas is naturally at ease and eloquent. Two years ago a stressful moment while speaking in public left him with occasional episodes of anxiety he cannot control. He wants the calm and lightness he had before back, as the pressure of his responsibilities grows. Please treat this with discretion.

Structuring his management

Organisation, planning and rigour are the areas he is working on, with support from people around him. A growing team needs clear rhythms, delegation and priorities.

Sustaining the pace

Growing pressure is part of the role. The work is about lasting energy and anticipating challenges early.

03 · What Thomas is looking for in a coach

The kind of coach that fits this moment.

  • Executive background: has held senior leadership roles and knows today's business reality first hand.
  • Empathetic, adaptable, and willing to challenge: a real listener who also pushes back. Thomas does not want soft consensus, and he does not want therapy.
  • Presence under pressure: has helped leaders regain composure and ease when presenting or facing high-stakes moments.
  • Manager-of-managers transition: has supported leaders stepping up to lead other managers.
  • French: coaching happens in French. All shortlisted coaches are fluent.
  • A plus: ease with fast-scaling, sales-led tech companies.

Thomas will meet two coaches and choose on chemistry. After the discovery call, he will receive a Wheel of Life exercise from Benoit, a self-assessment of eight dimensions of life, to bring more focus to the work.
