01 · Client context

Who Simon is and what they're stepping into.

Simon Travers, 28, is Head of Revenue Operations at FullEnrich, a French B2B SaaS that has grown from 20 to 60 people in a year (raised €2M, already breakeven). FullEnrich aggregates contact data from more than 20 premium vendors so revenue teams can find the emails and phone numbers others miss. Simon reports directly to the CEO, has two direct reports, and a team he expects to keep growing. The three cofounders run the company from San Francisco, and the most senior recent hires, a Chief of Staff and a CFO, are US-based too.

Simon is ambitious and clear about where he wants to be in five years: further up the executive ladder, most likely at this company. What worries him is the new senior layer forming above and around him. He can feel his seat at the leadership table becoming less certain, and he wants to earn it rather than lose it. He is also weighing a move to the US in a couple of years, though his English is not yet at a professional level.

02 · The coaching need

Four things this coaching has to move.

Standing among new peers

A Chief of Staff and a CFO have just joined above and around him. Simon wants to hold his place in the leadership team and be read as a genuine peer, not a function.

Managing up

He wants to keep reporting to the CEO and to carry that relationship with more authority and less deference.

Leadership posture

Building the seniority and presence of someone several years ahead of where he is now, while still only 28.

Growing his team

Two direct reports today and more to come. Shifting from doing the work to leading the people who do it.

03 · What Simon is looking for in a coach

The kind of coach that fits this moment.

  • Coaches in French — Simon is not yet fluent in English and wants to be coached in French.
  • Knows scale-up dynamics — has seen fast-growing tech companies up close and the politics of a leadership team forming in real time.
  • Works on posture, not just tactics — able to build genuine authority, presence and legitimacy, not only situational advice.
  • Revenue-function fluency, a plus — a read on RevOps and how a commercial org runs is a bonus, though not a prerequisite.
  • Available soon — Simon aims to start mid-to-late September, to be confirmed.
