01 · Client context

Who Bénédicte is and what they're stepping into.

Qonto is a European finance platform for SMEs, founded in 2017 by Alexandre Prot and Steve Anavi. The company serves hundreds of thousands of customers and is backed by €622m from investors including Valar, DST Global, Tiger Global, KKR and Insight Partners. After years of rapid growth, the company is now facing a sharp slowdown and declining revenues. The outgoing CFO is leaving, creating an immediate leadership gap in finance at a critical moment.

The CEO and co-founder Alexandre Prot needs a seasoned fractional CFO who can step in fast, stabilise the finance function, and right-size the cost base to match the new reality.

02 · The mandate

Four priorities that define this mandate.

Cost base restructuring

Realign the company's cost structure to reflect the current revenue trajectory, not the growth plan it was built for.

Finance leadership continuity

Step into the departing CFO's seat and keep the finance function running without missing a beat.

Cash and runway management

Give the CEO and board clear visibility on burn, runway, and the levers available.

Investor and board reporting

Own the numbers narrative with existing investors during a sensitive transition.

03 · What Bénédicte is looking for in an operator

The kind of operator that fits this moment.

  • Turnaround track record — Has restructured cost bases at least two or three times, ideally in high-growth tech companies that hit an inflection point.
  • Remote, cross-cultural fluency — Comfortable leading finance across distributed teams and multiple European markets from day one.
  • US market experience — Understands US investor expectations, reporting norms, and the lighter-touch regulatory landscape.
  • Immediate availability — Can start mid-September and operate at pace from week one.
