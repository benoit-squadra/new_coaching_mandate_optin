01 · The company

Software that understands human movement.

FitWise is an AI lab. Its model works on every human body, from video alone, with no sensors. Accuracy is documented at thefitwise.com/validation.

The team

Anna and Tatiana have built together for 13 years. They led the development team at OpenCV, the world's largest computer-vision library: 82M monthly downloads, 3B+ devices.

The market

Across sport, health, robotics, insurance and fitness, software that understands human movement is a $63B market, heading to $137B by 2031.

The beachhead: sport

Athletes are the most filmed and most valuable bodies on earth. FitWise starts with soft-tissue injuries, which cost football clubs $1.7B to $2.7B a year. PSG and the Houston Texans are in pilots.

The data edge

Every game feeds the dataset, now 2.6B 3D poses, 100× the largest existing set.
